NOTES FROM THE UNDERGROWTH

There is a whole world
beneath our feet.

After Rain is a small, living diorama. A place to slow down and notice the things we usually walk past.

  1. Carry a little light. Move across the forest, or touch and drag. Watch a soft glow travel through the moss. Keyboard explorers can use the arrow keys.
  2. Make a little rain. Touch the large leaf or choose “Let it rain”. Follow the droplet into the pool.
  3. Meet your neighbour. A snail is exploring the old bark. Move close and it will draw back. Give it a moment.
  4. Look again. Tap the lake for travelling rings, scroll or pinch to zoom, and use the compass button for two closer camera angles.

Scroll or pinch to look closer. Use the compass or “Reset view” to change the view.
